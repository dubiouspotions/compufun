[Office]
To undo or repeat several actions at the same time, click the arrow next to the Undo button or Redo button.

[Excel]
To insert subtotals into your list automatically, click any cell in the list, and then click Subtotals on the Data menu.
Microsoft Excel can open and save the following Lotus file formats: WKS, WK1, FMT, WK3, FM3, and ALL.
You can view two parts of the same workbook at once. On the Window menu, click Split to see both parts in one window, or click New Window to see each part in its own window.
