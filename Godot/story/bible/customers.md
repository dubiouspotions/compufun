# Customers

The people who come to the shop during the week. When each one comes is in the errands (`story/errands/`); who runs Cambion is in the table "Cambion, customer by customer" in [storyline.md](storyline.md). The moods each person has are on the wiki page Portrait framing.

## Middle manager
The shop's manager, leaving for vacation. Day 1 morning: the tutorial.

## Skater bro
Relaxed, says "dude". A buddy gave him Win95 Cambion on floppies, and his calculator answers 666. Day 1: dust in the fan. Day 4: leaves his computer for a defrag. Day 5: pops up behind the counter to check on it, and is hooked when he picks it up. Day 7: clearly corrupted, with a strange voice; he has replaced the parts with his own organs, and doesn't answer the phone again.

## Old dude
A retired accountant with tax returns and a nephew. The only one who brings the same problem again: no sound, on days 1, 2 and 3. On day 3 he also misses Clippy.

## Activist guy
Really a hacker; the player's character calls him the activist. Knows exactly what is wrong (day 1: bad RAM) and says little. He researches Cambion, and his computer holds hints of it. Day 6: he has seen what happens, leaves his computer and leaves town, telling the player to stay away from tech as well.

## Nice lady
General population. Day 2: her child's hamster crept into her computer.

## Young entrepreneur
"Time is money." His boot floppy brought Cambion (day 2), and Cambion glitches his voice. He pranks the office girl (day 3) and answers a scam email (day 4). Day 6: the player can delete him with a Win95 [x]. Day 7: if not deleted, he comes back in the office girl's place.

## Office girl
Pranked twice: inverted colours (day 3, by the young entrepreneur) and the screenshot desktop (day 5, by a colleague). Day 7: if the young entrepreneur was deleted, she comes without a computer, desperate.

## Hat guy
A know-it-all who won't listen and breaks his own computer: removes ".exe" from every program (day 3), swaps the parts around (day 5). Always grumpy at pickup. Day 6: infected, he shows off a computer with no parts that runs anyway, and leaves it for the player to look at.

## Attractive guy
General population. A jock who doesn't care about computers. Day 4: wants the screensaver his colleague has.

## Nice guy
General population. Day 5 evening: a broken hard drive, a calm errand.

## Top hat man
A rich mogul who loves how small and out of the way the shop is. Day 7: wants his hard drive replaced, won't say why, and insists that the old one is thrown away.
